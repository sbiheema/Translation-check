Translation Cross-Check

Add photos of the Arabic original and the sworn English translation. Dates, national numbers, passport numbers and every other number are compared character by character in code, so a misread digit cannot slip through as a "close enough" match.

Example data is loaded to show how a check looks, including two planted errors. Add your own photos or paste text to replace it.
